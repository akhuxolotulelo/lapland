What happens to your car

Five stages, about 13 minutes

Every wash follows the same order so grit comes off before a cloth ever touches the paint.

  1. 1 min

    Pre-rinse

    Loose dirt and road salt come off with low-pressure water.

  2. 2 min

    Snow foam

    A thick pH-neutral foam lifts the grit that is left.

  3. 5 min

    Hand wash

    Clean microfibre cloths, two buckets, panel by panel.

  4. 2 min

    Spot-free rinse

    Filtered water dries without leaving marks.

  5. 3 min

    Dry and finish

    Towel dry, windows polished, tyres checked.

Timings are for a Frost wash on a sedan.
